# Sanak Beauty

## Register

brand

## Users
Beauty shoppers exploring an original premium Indian cosmetics collection, on mobile and desktop.

## Product Purpose
A client-ready ecommerce demonstration with twelve products, useful shopping interactions, and a real 3D product studio. No real orders, payments or form submissions.

## Brand Personality
Warm, tactile, quietly confident. The supplied brief specifies editorial beauty photography, ivory, muted rose and burgundy, Cormorant Garamond and Manrope.

## Anti-references
Generic ecommerce templates, neon colors, excessive glass surfaces, crowded controls, fabricated certifications, and misleading customer endorsements.

## Design Principles
Give the product room. Make the next action clear. Preserve selected shades. Demonstrate real interaction. Keep all demo claims honest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA principles, keyboard navigation, modal focus management, reduced motion, labelled controls and non-drag 3D controls.
